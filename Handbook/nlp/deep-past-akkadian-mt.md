# Deep Past Challenge — Translate Akkadian to English — Top Gold Solutions & Post-Mortem

> **Competition**: [Deep Past Challenge - Translate Akkadian to English](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/deep-past-initiative-machine-translation) (Dec 16, 2025 – Mar 23, 2026 | 2,674 Teams | 68,346 Submissions | \$50,000 Prize Pool)  
> **Task**: Ultra-low-resource machine translation of **Old Assyrian (OA)** cuneiform transliterations (Bronze Age merchant letters, contracts, invoices) into single-sentence English  
> **Evaluation Metric**: Corpus-level (micro-averaged) **Geometric Mean of BLEU & chrF++** (`sqrt(BLEU * chrF++)` via `sacrebleu`)  
> **Dataset**: `600.78 MB` (10 CSVs: `1,561` **document-level** `train.csv` pairs, `7,953` untranslated `published_texts.csv`, `publications.csv` OCR from ~900 multilingual papers, `OA_Lexicon_eBL.csv`, host PDFs) | Hidden Test: **`~4,000` sentence-level rows** from `~400` tablets (`~34%` Public / `~66%` Private; `<= 9h` GPU/CPU notebook)

## 1. Universal Low-Resource MT & Data-Centric Extraction Breakthroughs

1. **Closing the Document-to-Sentence Mismatch via VLM Extraction & Iterative OOF Error Mining (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`, `#5`, `#8`, `#9`)**:
   - Generic OCR (Tesseract, EasyOCR, Docling, even domain-specific CuReD with `~92%` FP rate) mangled diacritics (`š, ṣ, ṭ, ḫ`) and superscripts (`#4`, `#9`). Combining layout OCR (`GLM-OCR`) with multimodal VLMs (`Gemini 3.0 Pro` / `3.1 Flash`, `Qwen3.5-Plus`, `GLM4.7V-Flash`) using **3-page sliding windows** (`#8`, since ~40% of translations spilled onto the next page) or **576-DPI overlapping chunks** (`#3`) cleanly unlocked 20k–163k sentence pairs across EN/DE/TR/FR volumes.
   - **OOF Data Debugging (`#1`)**: Running 4-fold OOF inference on the extracted corpus and flagging tablets with `geo_metric < 20` or length-ratio anomalies (`|len_diff| > 100`) drove the single cleanest Private LB gain (`data1 40.4 → data2 40.9 → data3+PL 41.6` on single `int8` `ByT5-XL`).
   - **Embedding-RAG Few-Shot Pseudo-Labeling (`#1`, `#3`)**: Grounding LLM pseudo-labeling of untranslated tablets with `NV-Embed` 8-NN retrieved examples jumped self-audited high-quality rate from **`61% → 82%`** (`#3`); training on RAG PL alone hit `37.1` Private LB (`#1`).
2. **Why Byte-Level Seq2Seq (`ByT5`) + `FP32` Crushed Subword/Decoder LLMs**:
   - All Top 10 teams centered on **`ByT5` (`base/large/xl`)** (supplemented by `mT5-Large` `#4` and `MADLAD-400-3B-MT` `#10` for cross-family diversity). Byte-level UTF-8 tokenization prevents subword fragmentation on diacritics, hyphens, and `<gap>` splits.
   - **Precision & Optimizer Stability**: `BF16` blurred byte-level diacritic distinctions (`š/s`, `ḫ/h`, `#9`) and Stage-2 fine-tuning needed **exact 1 epoch in `FP32`** (`#8`). Under `group_by_length=True`, default `Adafactor(β₁=None)` caused gradient spikes across length buckets — setting **`β₁=0.9`** eliminated instability (`#2`). During CPT, **Warmup-Stable-Decay (WSD) + `max_grad_norm=0.3` + AWP** tamed initial encoder norm explosions (`#3`).
3. **2-Stage `CPT → SFT`, Reverse Back-Translation, & Context/Gap Augmentation (`#3`, `#5`, `#6`, `#9`)**:
   - Controlled ablations (`#5` `ByT5-XL` Private): PDF-only `35.4` → `+CPT` **`38.9` (`+3.5`)** → `+external corpus` **`39.7` (`+0.8`)** → `+2-line [context]` **`39.8`** → `+100k reverse EN→AKK back-translation` **`40.7` (`+0.9`)**.
   - Keeping **5k CPT drill carryover** into Stage-2 SFT added another **`+0.4` Private** (`#3`); **Zipf Dynamic Context (`k_max=10`) + R-Drop (`α=0.8`) + PGD** (`#6`) and **distribution-matched mid-word `<gap>` injection** (`#9`) hardened models to damaged test tablets.
4. **Validation Stopping (`eval_loss` > `eval_bleu`), CTranslate2 Serving, & Source-Grounded MBR / LLM Reranking**:
   - **`eval_loss` vs Generation Metrics (`#1`, `#5`)**: `eval_bleu`/`eval_chrf` kept climbing past epoch 3 via style memorization while generalization degraded; stopping at **`eval_loss` minimum** prevented Private LB shake-down.
   - **CTranslate2 `int8_float32` (~5× speedup, `-0.1` cost, `#1`, `#5`)** + descending length-sorted batching unlocked 4–15 model MBR ensembles inside 9h on 2×T4.
   - **Cross-Model MBR & Reranking**: Diverse cross-model MBR added **`+0.6` (`#10`) to `+2.58` Private (`#9`)**; **Source-Grounded MBR (`SG-MBR`, `#9`)** combined `0.35·chrF_consensus + 0.10·lexicon + 0.55·source_invariants` (numbers, Sumerograms, divine names, `<gap>` parity); `#3` successfully used a fine-tuned **Qwen3-8B pairwise Reward Model** to arbitrate `ByT5-L` vs `ByT5-XL`.

## 2. Master Anti-Patterns & Empirical Negative Results

| Tested Hypothesis | Verified Impact | Teams Reporting | Root Cause |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Unsupervised DAPT Without Supervised Alignment / Warmup** | Dropped LB `38.5 → 34.6` (`-3.9`, `#9`); abandoned (`#1`) | #1, #9 | Unsupervised span corruption on noisy raw text misaligns encoder–decoder cross-attention |
| **Sampling-Only MBR (`T=0.7`) & Single-Model Multi-Checkpoint MBR** | Sampling MBR `+2.95` CV → **`-2.7` LB** (`#9`); single-model ckpt MBR `39.6 Pub → 38.4 Priv` (`#9`) | #6, #9 | Sampling introduces hallucinations that cluster artificially on CV; MBR requires true cross-model / cross-data diversity |
| **Rule-Based Post-Processing (N-Gram Dedup, Lexicon PN Replacement)** | N-gram dedup `41.5→41.6` Pub but **`43.2→42.8` Priv** (`#1`); PN dict crashed LB (`#2`, `#4`) | #1, #2, #4 | Polysemy (`a-na` = "to" vs PN "Anna") and inconsistent scholarly conventions make hard dictionary overrides destructive |
| **RL (`DPO`, `PPO`, `GRPO`, `MRT`) & Word-Level Dictionary Pairs** | Zero gain or degraded LB (`#2`, `#8`, `#9`) | #2, #8, #9 | Ultra-low-resource translation is data-coverage bound, not preference bound; isolated word pairs distort sentence syntax |
| **Standalone Decoder-Only LLMs (`Qwen2.5/3`, `LLaMA`, `TranslateGemma`)** | Trailed `ByT5` (best pure LLM finished 24th) | #1, #6, #24 | Subword tokenizers fragment cuneiform diacritics & hyphens; LLMs shine as data extractors, synthetic generators, or pairwise rerankers (`#3`) |

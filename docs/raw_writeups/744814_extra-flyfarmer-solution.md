<h2>Solution</h2>
<p><img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F1483555%2F35b8828b6cb7c16c602e0bcf834a48b3%2Fworkflow.png?generation=1790822444163641&amp;alt=media" alt="workflow"></p>
<ul>
<li>A rule-based agent plays the game.</li>
<li>Three circuits from the real fly brain (FlyWire) watch the game and step in only when they fire:<ul>
<li><strong>Startle:</strong> cash about to run out → buy fewer seeds</li>
<li><strong>Appetite:</strong> animal feed running low → pick up more wheat</li>
<li><strong>Learning:</strong> remembers whether sales went well (no decisions)</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2>The punchline</h2>
<p>A shuffled fake brain did just as well once I turned up its sensitivity.</p>
<p>So in the end, this is a rule-based agent with a fly squeezed in. The fly is basically a fancy alarm.</p>

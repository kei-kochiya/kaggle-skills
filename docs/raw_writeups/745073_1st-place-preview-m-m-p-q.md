### Message 1 by  (Votes: 107)

<p>Hi everyone! While we wait for the ratings to settle, here's a brief overview of our approach.</p>
<p>At different points during the competition, we reached <strong>#1 with agents from both our 10M- and 20M-parameter model lines, as well as with our standalone heuristic planner</strong>. We explored both training on more games and increasing model capacity. Ultimately, we chose a <strong>10M model with a rule-based action patch at inference time</strong>.</p>
<p>The three figures below illustrate how we got there:</p>
<ol>
<li><p><strong>Training loop</strong>
<img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F21381804%2F7a4bfd42e909b5faaba329258f991356%2Foverview.png?generation=1790912189252135&amp;alt=media" alt="">
We alternated behavior cloning (BC) with self-play PPO, learning from public replays and demonstrations generated by our heuristic planner. Replay analysis helped us identify weaknesses in the learned policy, improve the planner, and generate targeted demonstrations for the next training cycle.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Training history</strong>
<img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F21381804%2F77704d4aec4730d2721ec446c38f22a4%2Ftraining_detail.png?generation=1790912211323350&amp;alt=media" alt="">
This figure traces the repeated BC/PPO stages, the introduction of heuristic-generated demonstrations, and the parallel 10M and 20M model lines. The final 10M model's training lineage includes approximately <strong>8.29 million self-play games</strong>.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Architecture and inference</strong>
<img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F21381804%2F6b9020320d116e6238c57b0f7a98e1a6%2Fmodel_detail.png?generation=1790912225588059&amp;alt=media" alt="">
A shared Transformer predicts unit actions and market orders. Our chosen configuration, <strong>Final A</strong>, applies a rule-based action patch to resolve conflicts, avoid wasteful actions, and manage inventory. The figure also includes <strong>Final B</strong>, a variant trained with rule-aware PPO and sequential action masks.</p></li>
</ol>
<p>Our code is available on <strong><a href="https://github.com/msdsm/kaggriculture-1st-solution" target="_blank">GitHub</a></strong>.</p>
<p>Team contributions:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>morim3:</strong> Neural network architecture and the 10M model line, testing the hypothesis that more self-play games lead to stronger agents.</li>
<li><strong>msd0110:</strong> The 20M model line, testing the hypothesis that more parameters lead to stronger agents.</li>
<li><strong>Piiiiiiiii:</strong> Development of a strong heuristic planner.</li>
<li><strong>qistripute:</strong> Rule-based action patches and replay analysis, identifying weaknesses in our evolving strategies and suggesting improvements.</li>
</ul>
<p>We'll publish a detailed writeup once the ratings have converged and the final standings are confirmed.</p>
<p>Thanks to everyone who tilled the fields alongside us!</p>
